Terms of Bespoke Needlework Acquisition

Institutional contractual standards governing bespoke embroidery commissions, muslin prototype fittings, and salon appointments at Broderie Flair.

181 Mercer Street, New York, NY 10012, United States • +1-888-777-5845

1. Contractual Agreement & Atelier Commissioning

These Terms of Bespoke Needlework Acquisition govern all transactions, salon appointments, and custom embroidery commissions executed with Broderie Flair Atelier, operating from 181 Mercer Street, New York, NY 10012, United States. By confirming a custom Broderie Anglaise commission, scheduling a private fitting, or finalizing an order via +1-888-777-5845, the client enters into a binding contractual agreement adhering strictly to these terms, which supersede all prior oral negotiations and informal correspondence.

2. Natural Biological Variations in Hand Embroidery

Authentic Broderie Anglaise is an entirely manual artistic craft executed stitch-by-stitch by master needlewomen, meaning individual garments will exhibit subtle natural variations in stiletto tension, thread luster, and botanical spacing. While our artisans maintain rigorous symmetry and precision against archival prickings, clients acknowledge that slight manual nuances are authentic hallmarks of genuine haute couture craftsmanship rather than mechanical defects, affirming the unique heirloom value of each bespoke piece.

3. Commission Staging & Prototype Fitting Protocols

Bespoke embroidery production requires significant upfront commitments of hand-woven Swiss cotton batiste and eighty hours of specialized needlework. Consequently, a non-refundable commencement deposit of fifty percent is required upon pattern drafting, with the remaining balance due upon final salon fitting. For full bespoke garments, patrons must participate in a muslin prototype fitting to confirm anatomical proportions before our master embroiderers begin hand-piercing and stitching the final Irish cambric linen.

4. Delivery Schedules, Freight & White-Glove Transit

Standard delivery timeframes for bespoke commissions span six to eight weeks from prototype approval, subject to seasonal fabric weaving cycles and needlework complexity. All completed garments are packaged within breathable archival acid-free preservation caskets with contoured cedar hangers and dispatched via fully insured white-glove couriers requiring direct adult signature upon arrival. The atelier assumes complete transit risk until confirmed delivery to the client's verified address.

5. Inspection, Adjustments & Bespoke Return Exclusions

Clients are granted a seven-day inspection window upon delivery to review seam construction, buttonhole binding, and overall garment drape. Because bespoke garments are drafted to individual anatomical dimensions, custom commissions cannot be returned for cash refunds. However, if minor fitting adjustments are required, our Mercer Street atelier will execute necessary tailoring modifications without additional labor charges when notified within the initial seven-day inspection window.

6. Governing Law, Arbitration & Institutional Jurisdiction

These Terms of Acquisition shall be interpreted, construed, and enforced exclusively in accordance with the substantive laws of the State of New York, United States, without regard to conflict of law principles. Any dispute or claim arising out of a bespoke commission that cannot be resolved through amicable dialogue shall be submitted to confidential binding arbitration conducted in the County of New York, New York, under the auspices of the American Arbitration Association.
