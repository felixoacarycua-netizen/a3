Privacy Governance & Client Confidentiality

Institutional client records governance and bespoke needlework confidentiality standards at Broderie Flair Atelier, effective January 2026.

1. Foundational Commitment to Patron Confidentiality

Broderie Flair Atelier, operating our flagship embroidery salon at 181 Mercer Street, New York, NY 10012, United States, establishes this comprehensive Privacy Governance Charter to articulate our unwavering commitment to protecting patron personal identity records, bespoke anatomical sizing profiles, and bridal commission documentation. Because custom embroidery commissions require intimate anatomical fitting measurements, family heraldry notes, and private consultation records, our atelier manages all client interactions with institutional discretion, ensuring that patron data is never commercialized or exploited.

2. Direct Scope of Collected Patron Information

During private salon appointments or remote couture embroidery inquiries placed via telephone at +1-888-777-5845, we gather necessary contact credentials including full name, residential delivery address, telephone number, and secure email coordinates. Furthermore, our master embroiderers record specific anatomical dimensions including collar circumference, shoulder breadth, sleeve length, and custom monogram specifications. These records are retained securely within our encrypted atelier archives strictly to facilitate bespoke pattern drafting and future heirloom restoration services.

3. Cryptographic Security & Transaction Verification

Our digital web architecture utilizes TLS 1.3 cryptographic transport security protocols to protect all reservation requests and inquiry communications conducted across broderieflair.com. When patrons settle commission deposits or acquire salon accessories, transaction credentialing is processed directly through certified PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant financial gateways with tokenized verification. Our atelier servers never record, store, or process raw payment credentials or sensitive transaction credentials on local systems, ensuring total insulation against digital vulnerability.

4. Zero Data Commercialization & Trusted Logistics

We maintain a permanent institutional policy never to sell, rent, license, or barter patron personal records to commercial advertising aggregators, digital profiling platforms, or mass marketing brokers. Patron contact details are disclosed solely to bonded fine-art freight couriers requiring verified delivery coordinates for insured white-glove transit of completed heirloom garments, or when strictly compelled by valid judicial subpoenas issued by United States courts. All logistical couriers operate under binding non-disclosure covenants.

5. Patron Rights Regarding Sizing Archives

In full compliance with applicable United States and international data governance frameworks, every patron retains sovereign rights to access, inspect, rectify, or request the permanent deletion of their personal records and anatomical profiles. If you wish to review your stored measurement specifications, update shipping coordinates, or purge historical fitting records, you may submit a formal request to our Mercer Street salon concierge at concierge@broderieflair.com, where inquiries are resolved within thirty business days.

6. Inquiries and Institutional Supervisory Contacts

For formal inquiries regarding this Privacy Governance Charter or to exercise your statutory rights concerning personal data security, please contact our designated privacy compliance counsel at Broderie Flair Atelier, 181 Mercer Street, New York, NY 10012, United States, or contact our concierge desk directly at +1-888-777-5845. We review our digital encryption standards and data handling practices annually to guarantee enduring compliance with international consumer protection benchmarks while safeguarding our patrons' sartorial confidentiality.
