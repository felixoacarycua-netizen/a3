Atelier Product & Care Disclaimer

Technical guidance on delicate whitework characteristics, laundry methods, and proper textile conservation standards.

1. Nature of Broderie Anglaise & Needlecraft Literature

The historical treatises, needlework monographs, and fabric care guidance published on broderieflair.com by Broderie Flair Atelier, located at 181 Mercer Street, New York, NY 10012, United States, are provided solely for general educational and connoisseurial appreciation. While our master embroiderers strive for absolute historical and technical accuracy in textile literature, natural cotton and linen fibers remain organic biological materials subject to environmental aging and individual laundering factors that cannot be controlled with laboratory precision.

2. Laundering, Pressing & Mechanical Agitation Parameters

Although genuine hand-bound Broderie Anglaise possesses high tensile strength when properly maintained, it is a delicate heirloom textile that cannot tolerate harsh commercial washing machines, high-speed spin cycles, or chlorine bleach. Patrons are expressly cautioned never to wring embroidered lace or iron directly onto the face of raised satin stitches. Failure to follow our documented reverse-side pressing protocols on plush felted wool mats can permanently crush the sculptural three-dimensional relief of the needlework.

3. Fiber Characteristics & Environmental Exposure Disclosures

Our atelier works exclusively with pure botanical fibers, including long-staple Egyptian cotton batiste, Irish cambric linen, and filament mulberry silk. These natural cellulose and protein fibers naturally interact with ambient humidity, expanding slightly when damp and contracting when dry. Patrons should anticipate minor natural dimensional movement after laundering, which relaxes upon proper damp steam pressing and hanging on contoured cedar hangers.

4. Third-Party Alterations & Unauthorized Dry Cleaning

Broderie Anglaise garments must never be subjected to commercial dry cleaning solvents, perchloroethylene immersion, or industrial roller pressing, which dissolve natural fiber lubricants and flatten corded underlays. Broderie Flair Atelier disclaims all liability for shrinkage, thread breakage, or eyelet tearing resulting from alterations, cleaning, or restoration executed by unauthorized third-party dry cleaners lacking specialized museum textile conservation accreditation.

5. Sizing Guidance & Optical Color Representation

Digital photographs and color representations displayed on our website are calibrated to reflect true fabric tones under neutral daylight conditions. However, due to individual screen calibration variances and the natural light-scattering properties of fine openwork lace, physical garments may present subtle optical variances. Sizing charts serve as general anatomical approximations; bespoke commissions should be verified through private salon fittings or telephone consultations at +1-888-777-5845.

6. Institutional Updates and Inquiries

Broderie Flair Atelier reserves the right to modify, refine, or update this Product & Care Disclaimer at any time to reflect advancements in sustainable textile conservation and international couture standards. For technical guidance regarding the care, seasonal storage, or restoration of an heirloom whitework garment, patrons are encouraged to contact our Mercer Street master embroiderers directly at 181 Mercer Street, New York, NY 10012, United States, or via telephone at +1-888-777-5845.
